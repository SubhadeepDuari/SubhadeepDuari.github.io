about

Biology, read through models.

I'm a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Computational Biology at IIIT-Delhi, working at the interface of machine learning and experimental biology. My work builds interpretable models that read biological images and molecular data — from single-cell and nuclear morphology to cellular aging and ion-channel biophysics — and connects those predictions back to wet-lab experiments.

I trained as a biologist through the BS-MS program at IISER Bhopal, with hands-on electrophysiology, molecular cloning, and fluorescence microscopy. These days most of my time is spent in Python, turning microscopy and omics data into predictive, mechanism-aware tools — including contributions to platforms like scCamAge and AgeXtend.

I care about models that a biologist can actually interrogate: where a prediction comes from, which features drive it, and how it maps onto real cellular processes.

What I work on

Four threads that keep meeting each other — imaging, aging, biophysics, and generative design for molecules.

01

Computational pathology & nuclear morphometry

Interpretable representations of nuclei and cells from microscopy and H&E tissue — turning morphology into quantitative descriptors for classification and discovery.

image analysismorphometricsdeep learning
02

Aging biology & geroprotector discovery

Context-aware models of cellular age and aging-associated bioactivities across single cells, and explainable AI for surfacing candidate geroprotectors.

scCamAgeAgeXtendsingle-cell
03

Ion-channel biophysics

Mechanistic study of TRPV1 activation by the spider-venom double-knot toxin (DkTx), combining electrophysiology (TEVC) with biophysical modeling.

TRPV1electrophysiologybiophysics
04

Generative AI for drug discovery

Multimodal molecular representation learning and generative frameworks for protein degraders and mechanism-backed discovery of bioactive molecules.
